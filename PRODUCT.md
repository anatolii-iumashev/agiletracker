
# AgileTracker

## TL;DR

**AgileTracker** is a universal work management platform that combines the depth of Jira with the simplicity of Yandex Tracker — without the complexity of the former or the ecosystem lock-in of the latter. It handles tasks, projects, and business processes equally well for engineering teams, marketing, HR, and operations. A single tool to replace Jira + Yandex Tracker for teams that want power without the learning curve.

---


## Use Cases

AgileTracker works for IT teams and companies across any industry.

- **Agile Development & IT Projects**: sprint planning, backlog management, release tracking, bug and task management.
- **Product Management**: product backlog grooming and prioritization, roadmaps, version planning.
- **HR & Internal Processes**: recruiting, onboarding, leave management, sick days, business trips, and HR requests.
- **Customer Support & Service Desk**: ticket intake and processing, email integration, SLA tracking, and quality control.
- **Marketing & Campaigns**: campaign planning, budgeting, task coordination, and performance tracking.
- **Procurement, Document Flow & Operations**: approval automation, purchase management, contracts, and internal requests.
- **Classic Project Management**: Gantt charts, deadline control, resource allocation for teams of any type.
- **Distributed & Remote Teams**: transparency, seamless communication, and progress visibility.

AgileTracker is equally effective for small teams and mid-to-large companies that want one universal tool instead of two separate systems.

### Key Advantages

- **Universal** — works equally well for developers, marketers, HR, and operations staff.
- **Simple & Clean** — a modern, uncluttered interface that doesn't overwhelm newcomers.
- **Power Without Complexity** — Jira-level depth with GitHub Issues ease of use.
- **QR Task Creation**, AI assistant and macros — for maximum speed of work.

## Alternatives

AgileTracker competes with and replaces the following tools:

- **Jira** — the industry standard for Agile development, but notoriously complex, slow, and expensive at scale. AgileTracker offers comparable power with a fraction of the setup time and learning curve.
- **Redmine** — a classic open-source project tracker. Powerful but dated UI, requires heavy customization, and lacks modern collaboration features out of the box.
- **GitHub Issues & Projects** — tightly integrated with GitHub repos, great for developer-centric workflows. Falls short for non-engineering teams, advanced reporting, and cross-project visibility.
- **Yandex Tracker** — simple and practical, popular in Russia. Limited to the Yandex 360 ecosystem, lacks global integrations, and doesn't scale well for complex Agile workflows.
