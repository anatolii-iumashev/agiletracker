## Brand Voice
- Minimal, direct, no-BS
- Developer-first language — no marketing fluff
- Call out pain points honestly: "Jira is slow and bloated. This isn't."

## ICP
- Solo devs & freelancers → simple task tracking
- Small teams (2–15) → lightweight project management
- Mid-size teams (15–50) → Jira/Redmine alternative
- All: technical background, self-hosting comfortable, tired of overcomplicated tools

## Tone
- Confident, not arrogant
- Practical over inspirational
- Short sentences. Bullet points over paragraphs.

## Messaging Pillars
- **Simplicity + power** — minimal UI, zero bloat, but does everything you actually need
- **Open-source freedom** — MIT license, self-hosted, own your data

## Channels
- Primary: GitHub, Laravel News, Hacker News, Reddit (r/selfhosted, r/laravel)
- Secondary: Twitter/X, dev blogs
- No cold outreach

## Campaign Rules
- Every claim must link to a public demo or screenshot
- No "AI-powered" claims — product doesn't use AI
- Comparisons with Jira/Redmine must be factual, not dismissive

## Autonomy
- Content drafts: full autonomy
- Publishing: notify and wait
- Community engagement: human only
